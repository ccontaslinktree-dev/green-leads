- public/paes/kit-mockup.webp: 3D mockup generated for this project, transparent background. Illustrative covers; not a photo of finished printed products. Built-in image generation prompt: premium studio composition of two educational guidebooks (Matemática M1 and Competencia Lectora), a tablet and three small workbooks (100 Actividades Extra, Cuaderno de Errores, Plan de Repaso), navy/white/lime/orange infographic covers, Spanish titles, no price, people or official logos.
- public/paes/muestra-1..6.webp: actual pages rendered from the original six-page sample, not generated fake study screenshots.
- public/paes/muestra-paes.pdf: authored introductory sample only. No paid kit or 100-activity bonus is included in this commit.
- scripts/create-paes-sample.py: generate the sample and page images with ReportLab, PyMuPDF and Pillow. The hero mockup is a separate existing asset and is not regenerated by the script.
